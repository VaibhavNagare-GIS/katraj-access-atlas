How it works

  1. 01
    Keep every record

    14,191 buildings, 148 stop records, 4,888 road/path features.

  2. 02
    Measure in metres

    2D geometry in WGS 84 / UTM zone 43N. One internal point per building.

  3. 03
    Compare and intersect

    Nearest-stop distance, 500 m buffers, dissolved coverage and road intersections.

What this does not say

  • Not walking distance or travel time.
  • Not population served or ridership.
  • No service-frequency or crossing analysis.
  • Outside-ward stops are not included.
  • The boundary is from the 2017 resource.
  • 300 m and 500 m are exploratory bands.

148 stop IDs occupy 144 distinct coordinate locations. Shared coordinates are retained, not assumed to be duplicate records.

Data transparency

  • Statistics use the supplied PostGIS CSV exports.
  • Static derived geometry was reproduced from the supplied source files and checked against those exports.
  • Local source-order IDs are not asserted database keys.
  • All analysis layers load directly from bundled GeoJSON. No GeoServer connection is required.

No analytics, cookies or external font requests are added. OpenStreetMap tiles are requested on opening. Analysis geometry loads directly from this website.
